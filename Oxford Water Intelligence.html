Chance of spilling. For each overflow, a logistic regression on that day's rain, the previous day's rain, rain in the 6 days, the 23 days and the 60 days before that. The longer windows stand in for how wet the ground is and how high groundwater is; adding the Oxford borehole level on top made no difference. It is trained on 09:00-to-09:00 days from April 2022 to March 2025, using Thames Water's start/stop alerts, the nearest of four EA rain gauges, and skipping days the monitor was offline. Duration if it spills is a linear fit on the same inputs. Three sites with very few spills (Cassington, Tubney, Weston-on-the-Green pumping station) use a model pooled across all sites, scaled to their own spill rate.
