Model estimates, not measurements. The main model is a physics water balance of each treatment works: foul flow, rain off hard and saturated ground, and groundwater flow into the sewer; the works treats up to its capacity, a storm tank holds the excess, and only what the tank cannot hold spills. It is fitted only to each works' daily treated flow, never to its spill records, and scored on a test year it never saw (Oct 2025 to Oct 2026). Storm tanks hold 2 hours of flow at capacity unless a source states their size. Overflows with no treated-flow data use a statistical model of rain and wet ground as the backup. The rainfall slider reruns that day of the model with more or less rain. No public data measures spill volume, so these are estimates, and the glow is relative, not water quality. Data: Thames Water EDM, Environment Agency rain and flow gauges and LIDAR, OS Open Rivers (contains OS data © Crown copyright). Built at OxHack 2026.
