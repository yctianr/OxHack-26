How Spillcast works

A physics model of every sewage works around Oxford, tested on days it never saw.

← Back to the map

The model

Each works is simulated hour by hour as plumbing. Water arrives from homes, roofs and roads, saturated ground and groundwater. The works treats up to its capacity, a storm tank holds the excess, and only what the tank can't hold spills. It is fitted only to the flow each works treated, never to its spill records.

River quality

On a spill day, sewage from each works mixes into the river and dies off as it travels. Rivers with no works upstream are not assessed.

concentration = background + Σ works upstream: sewage strength × spill rate / (river flow + spill rate) × e−k × travel time
