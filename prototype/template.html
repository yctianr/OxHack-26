Why Oxford STW spills: flow into the works versus its capacity

Physical bucket model. Flow is split into household sewage, groundwater infiltration and rain runoff. A spill happens when the total tops capacity. Winter spills are mostly groundwater; this week's were rain on a rising water table.

Is the forecast any good?

Rolling backtest by hydrological year. Each model is trained on earlier years and scored on the next, then on this week's live event.
